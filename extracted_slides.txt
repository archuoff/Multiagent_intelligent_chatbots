EDS- Case study
Case study-
Use below examples to "fine-tune" or test your agent. These are designed for the technical depth of a mid-level engineer.
1: Automotive Plastics &amp; DFM
Engineer's Search:
 "What is the recommended rib-to-wall thickness ratio for a Class-A PP-EPDM bumper to avoid sink marks, and does this change if we use 20% talc filler?"
Agent's Expected Result:
 
"Per 
EDS Section 3.1 (Plastics)
, the standard ratio is 0.5. However, for 20% talc-filled PP, the EDS Guideline suggests reducing this to 0.4 due to increased shrinkage. 
Risk Alert:
 Exceeding 0.45 on a Class-A surface has historically caused a 15% rejection rate in the XYZ project.
2:Automotive Plastics &amp; DFM
Engineer Search:
 "Determining wall thickness for a PC/ABS instrument panel substrate near the airbag deployment zone. Provide aging impact.“
Agent Result:
Direct Answer:
 Base wall thickness must be 
2.5mm ± 0.15mm
.
Technical Constraint: Per EDS section 4.1
 In the deployment "hinge" area, thickness must transition to 
1.2mm via a 3:1 taper
 to ensure predictable ductile tearing at -30°C.
Validation:
 
Per EDS Section 6.1.2
, PC/ABS blends must have &lt;15% recycled content to prevent brittle failure during high-speed deployment after 1,000hrs of heat aging."
